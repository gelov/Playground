How to choose

Three questions before you pick.

How long is your day?

Work back from sunset, not from the summit. Add a buffer for photos, snacks and the walk back.

How did the last one feel?

Step up one level at a time. If your last hike left you tired but happy, you are ready for a little more.

What is the weather doing?

A moderate trail in clear skies can be harder than an easy one in rain. Read the forecast twice.
