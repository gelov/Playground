About Us

Real Estate Without Borders

Offshorly Properties connects homebuyers and international investors with residences and offshore estates that have been checked, priced openly and explained properly.

Who We Are

A small advisory team with a global network

We started Offshorly Properties to make buying across borders simpler. Too many buyers were left guessing about ownership, taxes and costs, so we built a practice around answering those questions first.

Today we work with private buyers, families and investors in more than 25 markets, with the same standard on every listing: verified, clearly priced and supported by specialists.

  • Every property reviewed before it is listed
  • Plain-language pricing and cost breakdowns
  • Legal and financial guidance in each market
