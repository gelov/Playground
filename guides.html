Start with your experience

Build confidence, one trail at a time.

The right route depends on you: how far you like to walk, how steady your pace is, and how much you have prepared. Match all three and a hard day becomes a good one.

Navigate the day

Know the route before you go.

Read the map, check daylight and weather, and agree a turn-back time before you set off. Turning around early is a good decision, not a failure.
