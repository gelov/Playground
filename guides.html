Hiking Guides

Build confidence, one trail at a time.

Match your pace, your pack and your plan, and a hard day becomes a good one.

01 · Experience

Start with where you are.

The right route depends on you: how far you like to walk, how steady your pace is, and how much you have prepared. Match all three and a hard day becomes a good one.

Easy
Short, shaded, clear paths. Good for a first outing.
Moderate
Steady climbs and longer days. A few outings behind you.
Hard
Exposed, long and committed. Start early, carry extra.

02 · Packing

Carry less. Be ready for more.

You do not need specialist kit to start. Good basics, packed with care, cover most day hikes.

Footwear
Broken-in shoes with grip. Spare socks.
Layers
Fast-drying layers and a waterproof shell.
Water
More than you think you need, plus snacks.
Emergency
First aid kit, headlamp and a whistle.
03 · Navigation

Know the route before you go.

Read the map, check daylight and weather, and agree a turn-back time before you set off. Turning around early is a good decision, not a failure.

04 · Common slips

Small mistakes that spoil big days.

Starting late
Leave with time in hand. Daylight runs out faster on the way back than it felt on the way up.
Cotton next to skin
Damp cotton chills you quickly. Choose layers that dry fast and add or remove them early.
One signal
Phones lose battery and signal. Carry a paper map and know how to read it.
